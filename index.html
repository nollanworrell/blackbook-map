⚡ What is Blackbook Map?

In graffiti and breaking culture, a Blackbook is a sacred archive—a sketchbook of styles, signatures, and history passed down between generations.

Blackbook Map is an open, community-driven living genealogy of breaking crews across the globe. It visually documents the lineage ties that link legendary pioneers to modern cyphers: who mentored whom, what crews merged, and which generation sparked the next.

🗺️ How to Use

🔍 Search & Filter

Use the top search bar to find crews by name or city. Filter by Active vs Inactive / Historic crews.

📍 Inspect Crews

Tap any crew pin on the map to open their dossier: home city, founding year, key founders, active eras, and social links.

🕸️ Follow Lineage Arcs

Selecting a crew highlights glowing arcs: Pink points to Heritage (parent crews) and Cyan points to Offspring (child crews).

➕ Add & Preserve History

Click + Add Crew to add your crew or link missing branches. Anyone can contribute to keep the record accurate.

🛡️ Wikipedia-Style Community Protection

Every creation, update, and lineage connection is recorded in the permanent Blackbook Changelog. If mistakes or vandalism occur, any member can restore or revert changes in 1 click.
